Roadmap at a glance

The sequence moves from understanding exposure, to proving solutions, to embedding resilient capabilities across IBM's operating model.

PHASE 1 · 2026–2028

Establish the foundations

  • Map critical workloads, regional dependencies and supplier exposure.
  • Set AI system inventories, risk tiers and verification requirements.
  • Measure energy, water, compute utilization and hardware lifecycles.
  • Run focused pilots for regional recovery, AI assurance and workload efficiency.
Phase outcome: A trusted baseline and evidence-backed pilot portfolio.
PHASE 2 · 2029–2031

Scale what works

  • Expand modular regional service patterns and tested recovery procedures.
  • Integrate AI validation, provenance and incident response into product lifecycles.
  • Scale workload orchestration, efficient cooling and hardware-life extension.
  • Compare results across regions and customer environments before wider rollout.
Phase outcome: Repeatable capabilities with measurable service, trust and resource benefits.
PHASE 3 · 2032–2046

Adapt and institutionalize

  • Operate regional services with shared security and interoperability principles.
  • Maintain continuous AI verification, recovery and transparent accountability.
  • Optimize infrastructure using both regional conditions and absolute resource limits.
  • Rebalance the portfolio as geopolitical, technological and resource conditions change.
Phase outcome: An adaptable business model that can operate through fragmentation and disruption.
Cross-cutting rule: Do not scale an initiative simply because a pilot works technically. Scale when it demonstrates customer value, operational resilience, security and measurable resource performance.

About this working canvas

This canvas brings together the five-year and twenty-year IBM scenarios and uses the Flourishing Business Canvas v2.1 as its organizing framework. The five-year Challenge Question is taken directly from the scenario. The twenty-year Challenge Question is a working synthesis, since the scenario does not state one verbatim.

The roadmap, priorities and suggested actions are analytical recommendations developed from the scenario material; they should not be read as announced IBM commitments. This is an editable study workspace. Check the canvas licensing terms before sharing a modified version publicly.
